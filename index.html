My research is on vision-language models and large language models: how they use visual and textual evidence, and how data and training can improve their reasoning and generalization. I am advised by Prof. Sungroh Yoon.

Recently, I studied how a model identifies the relevant image among many (NeurIPS 2026), and addressed CLIP’s failure to understand negation with synthetic data and fine-tuning (ICCV 2025).

More broadly, I am interested in visual and multi-image reasoning, grounding and hallucination, benchmarks and evaluation, and post-training methods such as reinforcement learning and on-policy distillation.
