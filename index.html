Junsung Park

Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering, Seoul National University

My research is on vision-language models and large language models: understanding how they use visual and textual evidence, and improving their reliability and generalization. I am advised by Prof. Sungroh Yoon.

I approach this from two directions. With interpretability, I study what a model actually relies on, most recently in multi-image reasoning (NeurIPS 2026). With synthetic data and fine-tuning, I address specific failures, such as CLIP's handling of negation (ICCV 2025).

Going forward, I plan to focus on post-training methods for vision-language models, building on co-authored work on reinforcement-learning-based post-training (ICML 2026). I have also taken part in industry-sponsored research with Samsung Electronics and SK hynix.
