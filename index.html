I am an AI researcher and a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Sungroh Yoon. I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University in 2022.

My research focuses on Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Large Language Models (LLMs), in particular on whether a model’s answer is grounded in the evidence it is given, such as an image or a retrieved document.

On the VLM side, I have studied how a model finds, among many images, the one a question refers to (NeurIPS 2026), how fine-tuning on synthetic captions improves CLIP’s understanding of negation (ICCV 2025), and how post-training can personalize a model’s responses (ICML 2026). On the LLM side, I have worked on multi-hop reasoning over misordered documents (NAACL 2025) and on using LLMs for interactive text-to-image retrieval (ACL 2024). My papers have appeared in NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ICCV, ACL, NAACL, and TPAMI.

I am currently interested in making VLMs reliable at understanding what they see. This includes visual and multi-image reasoning, grounding and hallucination, and benchmarks that measure these abilities. I am also interested in post-training methods such as reinforcement learning and on-policy distillation.
